# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language.
* Being respectful of different viewpoints.
* Accepting constructive criticism.
* Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual is officially representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions such as a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project participation depending on the circumstances and severity of the violation.
